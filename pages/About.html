COMPANY OVERVIEW

Focused on resources.
Committed to growth.

Alvala Company Limited is a Tanzanian company with a principal focus on mining and mineral processing, including gold recovery.

Its wider business outlook considers long-term development, responsible operational practices and constructive relationships with business partners.
